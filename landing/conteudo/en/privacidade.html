What data we collect

When you ask for contact on the site

Name, club name, WhatsApp, email, city and number of events per month. We also keep where your first visit came from, such as a Google search or a campaign link, and the first page you opened.

When you browse the site

We measure visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies. As on any website, the infrastructure provider records technical connection data such as the IP address.

When you use the system

Your login account data, such as name and email, and a log of accesses and activity in the system.

International transfers

Our data is hosted in Brazil, and some of these providers process data in other countries. If you are outside Brazil, your data is transferred to Brazil to provide the service. These transfers follow the safeguards set out in applicable law.

Cookies and browser storage

The public site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It keeps in your own browser, for up to 90 days, where your first visit came from, and this is only sent if you fill in the form. The free tools keep the timer structure in your browser without sending it to us. The system keeps session data on the device and, to work without internet, the entries waiting to sync.

Data of players registered in the system

Player data that a club registers in AceBlind belongs to that club, which decides how to use it. In those cases we act as a processor, following the club's instructions. If you are a player, talk to your club first.

How long we keep it

  • Contact requests: 24 months after the last contact, if you do not become a customer.
  • Access and activity logs in the system: 180 days.
  • Account data: while the contract lasts, and afterwards for the period required by law.

Your rights

You can ask us to confirm that we process your data, and to give you access, correct it, delete it, port it or tell you who we share it with. You can also object to processing or withdraw consent. Just write to privacidade@aceblind.net. You also keep the rights guaranteed by the law of your country, and can contact its data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this text when the service or the law changes. The date of the last update always appears on this page.
