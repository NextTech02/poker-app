How to use the timer at your tournament

  1. Check the structure. The timer opens with a sample structure. Under “Edit structure” you can change the blinds, ante and minutes of each level, and add or remove breaks.
  2. Put it on the TV. Connect your laptop to the TV in the room and click “Full screen”. The numbers grow so they can be read from a distance.
  3. Click Start. The sound plays in the last 10 seconds and at each level change. If you fall behind, use +1 min and −1 min to adjust the clock.

The structure and the current level are saved in your browser. If the tab closes by accident, open it again and carry on from where you stopped, adjusting the time if you need to.

No structure yet? The blind structure calculator works out the levels from the number of players and the length, and sends everything to this timer with one click.

Does the timer work without internet?

Yes. Once the page has opened, the countdown runs entirely in your browser, so an internet outage won’t stop the clock. Just don’t reload the page while you’re offline.

Frequently asked questions

Is the poker timer really free?

Yes. There’s no sign-up, usage limit or trial period. It runs in your browser, with no account and no server.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

It works in any current browser. For a tournament, a laptop connected to the TV is best, because the bigger screen can be read from a distance and the device stays plugged in.

Will the screen turn off mid-level?

While the clock is running, the timer asks the browser to keep the screen on. Not every device accepts that request, so check your power-saving settings before the tournament.

Can I use it with a big blind ante?

Yes. The Ante field for each level appears on screen alongside the blinds. Fill it in with the big blind amount and you have a big blind ante structure.
