Anyone who runs tournaments knows where the spreadsheet breaks: in the rebuy line, at the table that needs a new draw and in the payout math at two in the morning. AceBlind is live poker tournament software that follows the whole event, from the first buy-in to the last prize paid.

Blind structure and timer

Build the structure once and save it as a template for your next tournaments. Each level has blinds, ante and length, and breaks go into the sequence. To change the pace of the whole tournament, you copy the length to every level at once.

During the event, the tournament director starts, pauses, changes the level and adjusts the clock. The TV screen follows on its own, with an audible alert in the final seconds of the level.

Entries: buy-in, rebuy and add-on

Registrations, rebuys and add-ons are entered at the cash desk and count toward the tournament right away. You can limit rebuys by level, use a double buy-in and bring an eliminated player back with a rebuy. When registration closes, the director locks sales and the cash desk stops taking new entries.

Tables and seats

The seat draw works three ways: sequential, balanced or random. Tables, seats and eliminations are visible in real time, and a public screen shows every player where they’re seated.

Tournament formats

  • Multi-day, with real continuity between sessions and starting flights.
  • Satellite, with tickets tied to the main event.
  • KO, Progressive KO and Mystery, with bounties tracked by the system.
  • Team tournaments.

Payouts

The system applies the payout table for the number of entries with one click, and adds the tournament guarantee to the prize pool. Payment goes through the cash desk and generates a receipt. The free poker payout calculator uses the same table, if you want to check first.

Frequently asked questions

Does this poker tournament software work for a small club?

Yes. The Free plan covers 4 tournaments a month with 4 tables each, and Standard goes up to 20 tournaments a month with unlimited tables.

Do I need to install anything?

No. AceBlind runs in the browser, on the cash desk computer, a laptop or a tablet. If you prefer, you can install it as an app from the browser itself.

What if the internet drops mid-tournament?

On a screen that was already open, entries, eliminations and the timer keep working, and everything syncs when the connection returns. See what works and what needs internet on the offline poker software page.

Can I bring my player database over?

Yes. Your player records come in from a spreadsheet, and our team handles the migration. Re-importing won’t duplicate anyone already in.
