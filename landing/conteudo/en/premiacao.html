How payouts are calculated

  1. Collected: entries multiplied by the buy-in amount.
  2. House share: the rake or fee percentage on the amount collected.
  3. Prize pool: what’s left after the house share, plus the guarantee, if there is one.
  4. Places paid: taken from the payout table, based on the number of entries.
  5. Rounding: each prize is rounded, and the difference goes to 1st place. The total always matches the prize pool.

Rebuys count as entries. Two players with five rebuys between them pay out like a tournament of 7. If your tournament counts only buy-ins to decide how many places pay, adjust the Entries field.

Frequently asked questions

How does the guarantee work in the calculator?

The guaranteed amount is added to what the players paid, as it is in AceBlind. With 1,800 in net collections and a 5,000 guarantee, the prize pool is 6,800. If there’s no guarantee, leave the field at 0.

What house percentage should I use?

Use what your club charges. There’s no standard figure: it varies with the buy-in, the cost of running the operation and the region.

Why doesn’t 1st place get the exact percentage?

Because it takes what’s left over from rounding. That way nobody has to split cents, and the total paid equals the prize pool.

Can I download the payout table?

Yes. “Download spreadsheet” creates a file for Excel or Google Sheets, and “Print” produces a sheet with just the table.
