A 100% online system has one weak spot: without a connection, the screen stops. Installed programs don’t have that problem, but they’re tied to one computer. AceBlind is an online system that keeps what it needs on the device itself, so it can carry on operating during an outage.

What keeps working without internet

With your session open and the screen loaded before the outage, you can keep entering:

  • Buy-ins, rebuys and add-ons
  • Cash drops
  • Player registration and editing
  • Eliminations
  • Timer commands

Each entry is held in a queue on the device. When the connection returns, the system sends everything on its own, retrying every 15 seconds until it succeeds.

What needs internet

We’d rather tell you now than have you find out mid-event. These operations wait for the connection to return:

  • Logging in
  • Assigning players to seats
  • Paying out prizes
  • Using satellite tickets
  • Opening and closing a cash desk
  • Editing the blind structure
  • Cash game and bar

How to prepare for an outage

  1. Log in before the event on every device that will be in use, because login needs a connection.
  2. Leave open the screens you’ll use, such as the cash desk and tournament control. Without internet, a screen that was never opened only shows what was already stored.
  3. Open the cash desk before the tournament starts.
  4. Don’t reload the TV during an outage. An open screen keeps counting, but if it’s reloaded without a network it may lose the clock.

What happens when the internet comes back

Syncing is automatic, and each device has its own queue. If an operation can’t be accepted when it reaches the server, for example because the cash desk has already been closed, it goes to the conflicts screen: your team sees what didn’t go through and re-enters it by hand.

During an outage, each device runs its own clock. Timer commands apply to the screen where they were given and don’t reach the TV until the connection returns.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install it to work offline?

No. Working without internet applies to the system open in the browser. Installing it as an app is optional and only adds the desktop icon.

Is offline included in every plan?

Yes. Every plan, including Free, works offline and syncs on its own.

Can I operate all night without internet?

The entries listed above keep working, but the system was built to ride out outages, not to replace a connection. If your club’s internet drops often, a backup 4G SIM solves much of the problem.
