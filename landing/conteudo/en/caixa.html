A busy club night means money passing through several hands at once: front desk, cash desk, cash game and bar. When it all goes into a notebook or a spreadsheet, nobody owns the difference at the end of the night. AceBlind’s cash management records every movement under the name of the person who made it.

Cash desk by operator

Each staff member opens their own cash desk and works inside it. At closing, you see how much each person handled, in cash and in the other payment methods. A discrepancy stops being “someone made a mistake” and gets a name, a time and an amount.

Rake without mental math

Buy-ins, rebuys and add-ons have rake configurable by entry type. What goes to prizes and what goes to the house is split the moment the entry is recorded, not at the end of the month.

Cash drops and the main register

A cash drop takes money out of the operating cash desk and moves it to the main register, with a record of who did it and when. The night’s cash desk and the money already collected stay separate, so each balance is always visible.

Split payments

A player can pay part in cash and part by another method, in tournaments and in cash games. Each part goes into the cash desk under the right method, and the closing adds up.

Reports and P&L

The finance area gives you reports by period and by tournament, plus a P&L by module, so you can compare tournaments, cash game and bar. Reports export to CSV, XLSX and PDF, ready for your accountant.

Permissions and history

Permission groups define what each role can see and do. The activity log keeps logins and transactions for 180 days, so you can answer who changed what.
